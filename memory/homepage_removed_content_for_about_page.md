## Method — "How a single Compass interaction unfolds"
Intro copy: "Compass supports the relationship between a teacher and a learner. It teaches, rather than edits — the student always remains the author of their work."

4 steps:
1. **Understanding** — Compass first reads the writing as a reader would, to grasp what the student is actually trying to say.
2. **Recognition** — It names what the student has already done well — the productive move they have begun to make.
3. **Developmental invitation** — It chooses one instructional focus and invites the student to take the next thinking step themselves.
4. **Teaching** — It scaffolds that one move — never rewriting the work or supplying the ideas.
